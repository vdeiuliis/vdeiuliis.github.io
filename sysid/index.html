Model Identification Part
Lecture notes (30 hours) — from linear algebra and least squares to prediction-error and subspace identification
Based on the lecture notes by Vittorio De Iuliis · academic year 2024–2025
Interactive edition. Formulas are typeset with MathJax; hover a cross-reference to preview it. 14 interactive laboratories (sliders, draggable points, animations, live computations) accompany the salient topics of the course: the four fundamental subspaces, SVD and numerical rank, least squares, model validation and persistency of excitation, ARX and prediction-error methods, Ho–Kalman realization and MOESP. An animation in Chapter 1 retraces the loss and the rediscovery of Ceres.
