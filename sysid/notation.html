Notation

Sets, scalars, vectors and matrices

\(\N\), \(\N_0\)positive integers; non-negative integers (discrete time \(k\in\N_0\))
\(\R\), \(\C\), \(\R^n\), \(\R^{m\times n}\)real and complex numbers; real column vectors of size \(n\); real \(m\times n\) matrices
\(x\), \(A\)vectors are lower-case, matrices upper-case; \(x_i\) is the \(i\)-th entry of \(x\), \(a_{ij}\) the entry \((i,j)\) of \(A\), \(a_j\) its \(j\)-th column
\(e_i\), \(\mathbf 1\)\(i\)-th vector of the canonical basis; vector of ones
\(I_n\), \(0\)\(n\times n\) identity matrix (subscript omitted when clear); zero matrix or vector
\(A\T\), \(A^{-1}\)transpose; inverse of a square nonsingular matrix
\(A\pinvsym\)Moore–Penrose pseudo-inverse (Section 4.7)
\(\diag(d_1,\dots,d_n)\)diagonal matrix with the entries \(d_i\)
\(\bmat{A&B}\), \(\bmat{A\\B}\)block matrices (horizontal and vertical concatenation)
\(A(i\!:\!j,:)\), \(A(:,k)\)MATLAB notation: rows \(i\) to \(j\) and all columns of \(A\); all rows and column \(k\)
\(A\otimes B\), \(\vect(A)\)Kronecker product; vectorization (columns stacked one below the other), Section 8.6
\(\tr(A)\), \(\det(A)\)trace and determinant
\(\rank(A)\)rank of \(A\) (dimension of its image)
\(A\succ0\), \(A\succeq0\)symmetric positive definite, positive semidefinite
\(\delta_{ij}\)Kronecker delta (\(1\) if \(i=j\), \(0\) otherwise)

Subspaces, norms, decompositions

\(\spn\{v_1,\dots,v_k\}\)set of all linear combinations of \(v_1,\dots,v_k\)
\(\Im(A)\), \(\Ker(A)\)image (column space) and kernel (null space) of \(A\); \(\Im(A\T)\) is the row space, \(\Ker(A\T)\) the left kernel
\(\perp\), \(\oplus\)orthogonality; direct sum of subspaces
\(\ip{x}{y}=x\T y\), \(\norm{x}\)Euclidean inner product and norm \(\norm{x}=\norm{x}_2=\sqrt{x\T x}\)
\(\norm{A}_2\), \(\norm{A}_F\)spectral norm (\(=\sigma_1\)) and Frobenius norm of a matrix
\(\Pi_{\mathcal V}\)orthogonal projector onto the subspace \(\mathcal V\); \(\Pi_{\Im(A)}=AA\pinvsym\)
\(\Pip\)in Chapter 8, orthogonal projector onto \(\Ker(U_{0,s,\ell})\)
\(\lambda_i\), \(r_i\), \(l_i\T\)eigenvalues, right and left eigenvectors; \(A=R\Lambda L\) (Theorem 2.8)
\(m_{\mathrm{alg}}\), \(m_{\mathrm{geo}}\)algebraic and geometric multiplicity of an eigenvalue
\(\rho(A)\)spectral radius \(\max_i|\lambda_i(A)|\)
\(A=U\Sigma V\T\)singular value decomposition; \(\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\dots\ge0\) singular values; \(U_r,\Sigma_r,V_r\) its rank-\(r\) part (Section 3.3)
\(\kappa(A)\)condition number \(\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}\)
\(A=QR\), \(A=LQ\)QR and LQ factorizations (Sections 2.6 and 8.7)

Probability and estimation

\(\E(\cdot)\), \(\operatorname{cov}(\cdot)\)expected value; covariance matrix
\(\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^2)\)Gaussian distribution with mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\sigma^2\)
\(\hat\theta\), \(\hat x\), \(\hat A\)estimates (“hat”) of the corresponding true quantities \(\theta_0\), \(x_0\), \(A\)
\(\argmin\), \(\argmax\)minimizer, maximizer of a function
\(\varepsilon\)residual \(y-Ax\) (Chapter 4) or prediction error \(y(k)-\hat y(k|k-1)\) (Chapter 6)
\(\hat y(k|k-1)\)one-step-ahead prediction of \(y(k)\) given the data up to time \(k-1\)
\(\varphi(k)\), \(\Phi\)regressor vector and regressor matrix of a linear regression \(Y=\Phi\theta\)
\(V_N(\theta)\)loss function (sample mean of the squared prediction errors on \(N\) data)
\(\lambda\)regularization parameter in ridge regression (Section 4.10); also eigenvalue
MSE, RMSE, FITerror measures (Section 5.4); AIC, FPE, MDL order-selection criteria (Section 6.6)

Signals and systems

\(n\), \(p\), \(q\)always: number of states, of inputs and of outputs
\(x(k)\in\R^n\), \(u(k)\in\R^p\), \(y(k)\in\R^q\)state, input and output at the discrete time \(k\)
\(A,B,C,D\)state-space matrices of \(x(k+1)=Ax(k)+Bu(k)\), \(y(k)=Cx(k)+Du(k)\); \(x(0)=x_0\) initial state
\(T\)nonsingular change of coordinates, \(x=T\tilde x\): \(\{T^{-1}AT,T^{-1}B,CT,D\}\)
\(v(k)\), \(e(k)\)measurement noise; white noise (innovation) with variance \(\sigma^2\) or \(\lambda_e\)
\(\delta(k)\)discrete impulse: \(\delta(0)=1\), \(\delta(k)=0\) for \(k\neq0\)
\(w(k)\), \(W(k)\)impulse response (SISO) and impulse response matrix (MIMO, \(q\times p\)); \(W(k)=CA^{k-1}B\) for \(k\ge1\) are the Markov parameters
\(\Z\{\cdot\}\), \(z\)\(\Z\)-transform, complex variable; \(F(z)=\sum_{k\ge0}f(k)z^{-k}\)
\(W(z)\)transfer function / matrix \(C(zI-A)^{-1}B+D\)
\(q\), \(q^{-1}\)forward and backward shift operators: \(q^{\tau}f(k)=f(k+\tau)\), \(q^{-\tau}f(k)=f(k-\tau)\) for \(\tau\in\N\) (e.g. \(q^{-1}f(k)=f(k-1)\), one-step delay) (Section B.1)
\(A(q),\dots,F(q)\)polynomials in \(q^{-1}\) of the polynomial models (Chapter 6 and Appendix B); \(A(q)=1-a_1q^{-1}-\cdots\) (MATLAB: \(1+a_1q^{-1}+\cdots\))
\(W(q)\), \(H(q)\)plant and noise transfer operators: \(y(k)=W(q)u(k)+H(q)e(k)\)
\(n_a,n_b,n_c,n_d,n_f,n_k\)numbers of coefficients of the polynomials; input delay
\(\Rch\), \(\Obs\)reachability and observability matrices \([B\;AB\;\cdots\;A^{n-1}B]\), \([C;\,CA;\,\dots;\,CA^{n-1}]\)
\(\Rch_\ell\), \(\Obs_s\)extended reachability matrix (\(\ell\) block columns) and extended observability matrix (\(s\) block rows)
\(\Toe_s\)block lower-triangular Toeplitz matrix of the Markov parameters (Chapter 8)
\(H_{s,\ell}\)block Hankel matrix of the Markov parameters, \(s\) block rows and \(\ell\) block columns (Chapter 7)
\(U_{i,s,\ell}\), \(Y_{i,s,\ell}\)block Hankel matrices of input and output data: first sample \(u(i)\), \(s\) block rows, \(\ell\) columns (Definition 5.1)
\(X_{i,\ell}\)state sequence \([x(i)\;x(i+1)\;\cdots\;x(i+\ell-1)]\)
\(L^{\up}\), \(L^{\dn}\)a matrix \(L\) without its last block row and without its first block row (shift invariance)
\(N\)number of available data samples; \(s,\ell\) user-chosen dimensions of the Hankel matrices
