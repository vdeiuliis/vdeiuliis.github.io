My first name is *Vittorio*, and my last name (family name) is *De Iuliis* (pronounced like DE - YOU - LEE - IS, [listen](https://en-audio.howtopronounce.com/1646225041621f66914b78e.mp3)) with no middle name.
My birthplace is [**Vasto**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasto), a small ancient town in the wonderful [**Abruzzo**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abruzzo) region, in central Italy. Vasto is the center of the [**Trabocchi coast**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trabocchi_Coast), a beautiful short coastline dotted with peculiar fishing machines consisting of wooden platforms anchored to the rocks. Vasto is also known for its [**beautiful shores**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3QH24qPG5w) and its culinary delights. Among these, the famous [**"brodetto alla vastese"**](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2008/11/24/one-fish-two-fish) is the one to go for. I spent my first childhood in [**Castiglione Messer Marino**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castiglione_Messer_Marino), a gracious little village in the southern mountain part of Abruzzo. [**Juan Manuel Fangio**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Fangio), the famous Formula 1 champion, had his roots in Castiglione.
