My first name is Vittorio and my family name is De Iuliis ([listen](https://en-audio.howtopronounce.com/1646225041621f66914b78e.mp3)). No middle name.

I was born in [Vasto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasto), a small and ancient town in [Abruzzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abruzzo), central Italy. Vasto is the heart of the [Trabocchi Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trabocchi_Coast), named after the wooden fishing platforms anchored to its rocks. It has [beautiful shores](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3QH24qPG5w) and a great cuisine: if you ever pass by, try the [brodetto alla vastese](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2008/11/24/one-fish-two-fish).

I spent my early childhood in [Castiglione Messer Marino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castiglione_Messer_Marino), a village in the southern mountains of Abruzzo, where Formula 1 champion [Juan Manuel Fangio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel_Fangio) had his roots.

Over the years I have had many passions: literature, [music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76o5q4zhjRk), [cinema](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ak6rI-j07QU), theater, [photography](https://www.instagram.com/vittoriodeiuliis/), [billiards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9D2rFMPN9js), wristwatches, [wine](https://dinersjournal.blogs.nytimes.com/2010/06/07/the-wines-of-valentini/), table tennis, [comic strips](https://www.gocomics.com/peanuts/2019/09/14), [journalism](http://www.ilcorrieremusicale.it/author/vittoriodeiuliis/), and cappuccino tasting.

Then I discovered the joy of teaching, and all the rest moved to the "hobbies" section of my CV (which, by the way, I don't like — [here](https://tryinagain.wordpress.com/2008/06/27/writing-a-resume-wislawa-szymborska/) is why).

A professional timewaster.
