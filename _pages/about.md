I am an **Associate Professor** at [San Raffaele University of Rome](https://www.uniroma5.it/), in the Department for the Promotion of Human Science and Quality of Life, and an **Adjunct Professor** at the [University of L'Aquila](https://www.univaq.it/).

My research lies in **systems and control theory**, with a focus on time-delay systems, positive systems, switched and Markov jump systems, system identification, and the use of machine learning in control.
